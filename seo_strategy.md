# SEO Strategy — Evening Sun Eatery & Supermarket

## In scope
- Public single-page website (`/`) — the main landing page served to all visitors

## Out of scope
- Admin API endpoints (`/api/admin/**`) — authenticated, not public-facing
- Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions overlays — client-rendered state-based pages, no independent URL

## Target audience
- Local customers in Mosafejo / Badagry area, Lagos State, Nigeria
- People searching for restaurants, eateries, bakeries, grill spots, lounges, supermarkets in the area

## Primary keywords
- Evening Sun Eatery Mosafejo
- Restaurant Badagry Nigeria
- Nigerian food Mosafejo
- Eatery & Supermarket Badagry

## Site classification
- Pure React + Vite SPA (Single Page Application)
- No SSR / SSG — all content is client-rendered
- Single public page (no multi-page routing)

## Crawler assumptions
- Googlebot may eventually render the JS; other crawlers (social bots, AI crawlers) typically do not
- Local business SEO is the primary concern — Google Business Profile + LocalBusiness schema
